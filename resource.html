Frontier-paper checklist

  • Confirm current title, author list, venue, and latest revision.
  • Check whether code, data, weights, and evaluation scripts are public.
  • Audit baseline selection, compute accounting, data overlap, and benchmark construction.
  • Look for independent reproduction or follow-up evidence.
  • Decide whether the paper should remain Frontier, move into the main schedule, or return to backup.
