How bundles work

One slot, one argument

The presenter owns the lead paper and uses companions only to make the methodological change legible.

1

Lead · full read

Understand the method, evidence, failure cases, and assumptions well enough to teach and critique it.

2

Skim · compare

Read only the sections needed to explain the lineage, competing representation, or evaluation contrast.

3

Discuss · synthesize

End with the real research question: what changed, what improved, and what remains unproven?
