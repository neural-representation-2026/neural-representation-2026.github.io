Format

This is a reading and discussion seminar. Students are expected to read the assigned papers before class and participate actively in discussion.

Part 1: Presentation (50%)

Each participant gives an approximately 25-minute presentation on one paper or one paper bundle. For a bundle, present the lead paper in detail and use the remaining papers for comparison, context, or discussion.

  • Form (30%): timing, clarity, delivery, and engagement
  • Content (50%): structure, main ideas, paper connections, and conclusions
  • Answers (20%): command of the topic and responses to questions

Part 2: Discussion (25%)

Presentations are followed by a class discussion of strengths, weaknesses, open questions, and future directions. Question-submission instructions will be posted before the first student presentation.

Part 3: Other Participation (25%)

This component includes regular participation and any special lectures, hands-on activities, favorite-paper presentations, or end-of-semester brainstorming announced by the instructor.
