Preparation · presentation · discussion

Seminar format

The goal is not to summarize every paragraph. A strong presentation reconstructs the paper’s argument, identifies its decisive assumptions, tests its evidence, and connects it to the method family.

The bundle ruleOne lead paper is read deeply. At most two companions are skimmed for a specific comparison. A bundle is still one signup and one presentation slot.

Suggested class rhythm

  • Topic framing: 5–10 minutes by the instructor or TA.
  • Student presentations: approximately 25 minutes per assigned reading slot.
  • Discussion: compare assumptions, evidence, failures, and future work across the week.

Before class

  • Read the lead paper for each assigned slot. For bundles, skim only the listed comparison papers.
  • Prepare at least one substantive question per reading slot; avoid questions answered directly in the abstract.
  • For a Frontier paper, verify the current version, review status, code/data release, baselines, compute, and known failure cases.
  • Presenters should upload slides by the deadline set by the teaching team.

Presentation structure

  1. Problem and stakes: What bottleneck or capability is the paper actually about?
  2. Necessary background: Introduce only the concepts needed to follow the method.
  3. Method: Make the representation, objective, supervision, and inference procedure explicit.
  4. Evidence: Separate quantitative claims, qualitative demonstrations, ablations, and system-level evidence.
  5. Bundle comparison: State exactly what changed from the companion paper and why it matters.
  6. Limitations: Include failure modes, missing comparisons, compute/data constraints, and external-validity risks.
  7. Discussion hook: End with the question listed for the slot—or improve it.

Reading different slot types

Classic bundle

Use the lead classic to understand the field-defining representation. Skim companions for the transition in encoding, rendering, geometry, or systems design. Do not give three mini-book reports.

Mixed-lineage bundle

The recent paper is the center. The historical paper exists to reveal what moved into a pretrained prior, what became amortized, and what classical optimization still contributes.

Recent bundle

Compare two closely related 2025/26 methods around outputs, assumptions, evaluation protocol, scale, and failure cases—not just benchmark deltas.

Frontier paper

Treat the presentation as a conference review. Clearly distinguish reported evidence from your inference and from claims that remain unverified.

Discussion and feedback

  • Use submitted questions to probe the paper’s assumptions rather than to repeat presentation content.
  • Connect papers within the week and to earlier weeks when representations or evaluation choices recur.
  • Ask whether improvements survive different data, sensors, compute budgets, camera models, or downstream robot tasks.
  • Conclude with one strength, one limitation, and one concrete next experiment.

Provisional evaluation dimensions

  • Communication: timing, clarity, visual design, and audience engagement.
  • Technical content: method correctness, evidence, structure, and bundle synthesis.
  • Critical analysis: limitations, answers to questions, and quality of proposed follow-up work.
  • Participation: submitted questions and constructive discussion across the semester.
